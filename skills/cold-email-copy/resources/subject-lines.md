# Subject Lines

## Contract

- Use three to five words.
- Make the topic clear.
- Use ordinary capitalization and punctuation.
- Do not use false urgency, clickbait, or a fake internal thread.
- Do not use unsupported facts or personal claims.
- Make the subject and first line form one coherent thought.

## Safe patterns

- Workflow: `customer handoff notes`
- Role: `revenue operations systems`
- Artifact: `the routing checklist`
- Question: `renewal risk ownership`
- Event: `after the team expansion`
- Outcome: `fewer manual reviews`

These are structural examples, not claims. Use an event pattern only when the event is verified.

## Reject

- Subjects longer than five words.
- One-word subjects with no context.
- All caps or repeated punctuation.
- Words such as urgent, exclusive, guaranteed, or last chance without a valid operational reason.
- Personal details that do not affect the business topic.
- Subjects designed only to improve an open metric.

Return two or three subject options unless the user asks for one.
