# Reply Copy

Replies are a separate format. They have no sequence word limit and never use spintax.

## Positive reply workflow

1. Acknowledge the exact interest or request.
2. Answer the immediate question first.
3. Provide the promised item or state when it will arrive.
4. Route to one next action that matches the prospect's intent.
5. Keep all new factual claims sourced.

## CTA routing

- If the prospect asks for material, send it before asking for a meeting.
- If the prospect asks a factual question, answer it before proposing a next step.
- If the prospect wants to talk, offer a specific scheduling path.
- If ownership is wrong, thank them and ask for the correct role or contact.
- If timing is wrong, confirm a reasonable follow-up date.

## Recovery replies

### Missing evidence

State what cannot be confirmed and what source is needed. Do not guess to keep momentum.

### Misunderstood request

Briefly restate the corrected need, answer it, and avoid defending the earlier message.

### Objection

Acknowledge the objection without pressure. Add one relevant fact only when it directly resolves the
concern. Otherwise close politely.

### Not interested

Respect the answer. Do not add a hidden second pitch. Confirm that no further follow-up is needed.

## Reply checks

- The first sentence responds to the prospect's message.
- The reply contains one next action.
- Promised material is attached, linked, or given a clear delivery time.
- No new unsupported claim appears.
- No sequence limits or spintax are applied.
