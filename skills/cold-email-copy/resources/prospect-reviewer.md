# Prospect Clarity Reviewer

Review the canonical draft from the recipient's point of view.

Check:

1. The first lines make the topic and relevance clear.
2. The problem is recognizable without inside knowledge.
3. The outcome is concrete and easy to understand.
4. The CTA asks for one low-effort action.
5. The language fits the stated register and reading level.
6. The copy shows knowledge of an expert's work without explaining that work back to them.
7. A personalized opener uses a verified usable anchor or the approved segment fallback.

Return at most five findings. Each finding is one short sentence with a location, issue, and proposed
change. Quote no more than twelve draft words. Do not copy the draft. Mark the review `pass` when no
change is necessary.
